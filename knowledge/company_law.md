# Important Provisions of Chinese Company Law

## Company Establishment Conditions
Article 23 of the "Company Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that the following conditions shall be met for establishing a limited liability company:
1. The shareholders shall meet the statutory number;
2. The capital contribution of shareholders shall reach the statutory minimum amount;
3. Shareholders shall jointly formulate the company's articles of association;
4. The company shall have a name and establish an organizational structure that meets the requirements of a limited liability company;
5. The company shall have a registered address.

Article 76 stipulates that the following conditions shall be met for establishing a company limited by shares:
1. The promoters shall meet the statutory number;
2. The share capital subscribed and raised by the promoters shall reach the statutory minimum amount;
3. The share issuance and preparation matters shall comply with legal provisions;
4. The promoters shall formulate the company's articles of association, and if established by means of share offering, it shall be approved by the inaugural meeting;
5. The company shall have a name and establish an organizational structure that meets the requirements of a company limited by shares;
6. The company shall have a registered address.

## Corporate Governance Structure
Article 37 of the "Company Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that the shareholders' meeting of a limited liability company shall consist of all shareholders. The shareholders' meeting is the company's authority, exercising powers in accordance with this law.

Article 45 stipulates that a limited liability company shall establish a board of directors consisting of three to thirteen members; however, exceptions apply as provided in Article 50 of this law. For limited liability companies established by two or more state-owned enterprises or two or more other state-owned investment entities, employee representatives shall be included in the board of directors; other limited liability companies may include employee representatives on their board of directors. Employee representatives on the board of directors shall be democratically elected by company employees through employee representative assemblies, employee assemblies, or other forms. The board of directors shall have one chairperson and may have one vice chairperson. The method for appointing the chairperson and vice chairperson shall be specified in the company's articles of association.

Article 51 stipulates that a limited liability company shall have a manager, who shall be appointed or dismissed by the board of directors. The manager shall be responsible to the board of directors and exercise the following powers:
1. Managing the company's production and operations, and implementing the resolutions of the board of directors;
2. Implementing the company's annual business plans and investment plans;
3. Drafting plans for the establishment of the company's internal management structure;
4. Drafting the company's basic management systems;
5. Formulating the company's specific rules and regulations;
6. Recommending the appointment or dismissal of deputy managers and financial officers;
7. Deciding on the appointment or dismissal of management personnel other than those whose appointment or dismissal is to be decided by the board of directors;
8. Other powers granted by the board of directors.

## Shareholder Rights and Obligations
Article 4 of the "Company Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that company shareholders shall legally enjoy rights such as returns on assets, participation in major decisions, and selection of managers.

Article 34 stipulates that shareholders shall receive dividends in proportion to their paid-in capital contribution; when the company increases its capital, shareholders shall have the right to preferentially subscribe for capital contribution in proportion to their paid-in capital. However, this does not apply if all shareholders agree not to distribute dividends in proportion to capital contribution or not to preferentially subscribe for capital contribution in proportion to paid-in capital.

Article 97 stipulates that the shareholders' meeting of a company limited by shares shall exercise the following powers:
1. Deciding on the company's business policies and investment plans;
2. Electing and replacing directors and supervisors who are not employee representatives, and deciding on matters concerning the remuneration of directors and supervisors;
3. Reviewing and approving reports of the board of directors;
4. Reviewing and approving reports of the board of supervisors or supervisors;
5. Reviewing and approving the company's annual financial budget plans and final account plans;
6. Reviewing and approving the company's profit distribution plans and plans for covering losses;
7. Making resolutions on the increase or reduction of the company's registered capital;
8. Making resolutions on the issuance of corporate bonds;
9. Making resolutions on matters such as the company's merger, division, dissolution, liquidation, or change of company form;
10. Amending the company's articles of association;
11. Other powers stipulated in the company's articles of association.

## Company Registered Capital
Article 26 of the "Company Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that the registered capital of a limited liability company shall be the amount of capital contribution subscribed by all shareholders as registered with the company registration authority. Where laws, administrative regulations, and decisions of the State Council provide otherwise for the paid-in registered capital and minimum amount of registered capital of limited liability companies, such provisions shall prevail.

Article 80 stipulates that for a company limited by shares established by promotion, the registered capital shall be the total share capital subscribed by all promoters as registered with the company registration authority. Before the shares subscribed by the promoters are fully paid, shares shall not be offered to others. For a company limited by shares established by share offering, the registered capital shall be the total paid-in share capital registered with the company registration authority. Where laws, administrative regulations, and decisions of the State Council provide otherwise for the paid-in registered capital and minimum amount of registered capital of companies limited by shares, such provisions shall prevail.

## Company Merger, Division, and Dissolution
Article 173 of the "Company Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that a company merger may take the form of absorption or consolidation. The absorption of one company by another company is a merger by absorption, in which the absorbed company is dissolved. The merger of two or more companies to establish a new company is a merger by consolidation, in which the merging parties are dissolved.

Article 175 stipulates that when a company is divided, its property shall be divided accordingly. When a company is divided, it shall prepare a balance sheet and an inventory of property. The company shall notify its creditors within ten days from the date of the division resolution and shall publish an announcement in a newspaper within thirty days.

Article 180 stipulates that a company shall be dissolved for the following reasons:
1. The term of business operation specified in the company's articles of association has expired, or other causes for dissolution as specified in the company's articles of association have occurred;
2. The shareholders' meeting or the general shareholders' meeting has resolved to dissolve the company;
3. Dissolution is necessary due to the merger or division of the company;
4. The company's business license has been revoked, or the company has been ordered to close or has been canceled according to law;
5. The people's court orders the company to dissolve in accordance with the provisions of Article 182 of this law.
