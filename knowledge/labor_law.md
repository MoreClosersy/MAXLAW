# Important Provisions of Chinese Labor Law

## Basic Labor Contract Provisions
Article 10 of the "Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that a written labor contract shall be concluded in the establishment of an employment relationship. If a written labor contract is not concluded simultaneously with the establishment of an employment relationship, a written labor contract shall be concluded within one month from the date of employment.

Article 14 stipulates that an open-ended labor contract is a labor contract for which the employer and the employee have agreed not to set a definite ending date. Under any of the following circumstances, if the employee proposes or agrees to renew or conclude a labor contract, an open-ended labor contract shall be concluded, unless the employee requests the conclusion of a fixed-term labor contract:
1. The employee has worked continuously for the employer for a full 10 years;
2. When the employer initially implements the labor contract system or the employer is a state-owned enterprise that is undergoing restructuring and is re-concluding labor contracts, the employee has worked continuously for the employer for a full 10 years and is less than 10 years away from the statutory retirement age;
3. A fixed-term labor contract has been concluded for two consecutive terms, and the employee does not fall under the circumstances specified in Article 39 and Article 40, items (1) and (2) of this Law, if the labor contract is to be renewed.

## Labor Remuneration Provisions
Article 46 of the "Labor Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that the distribution of wages shall follow the principle of distribution according to work and equal pay for equal work. The wage level shall be gradually increased on the basis of economic development. The state shall implement macro-control over the total amount of wages.

Article 47 stipulates that the employer shall, in accordance with the law, independently determine its mode of wage distribution and wage level based on its production and operation characteristics and economic results.

## Working Hours and Rest and Leave
Article 36 of the "Labor Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that the state shall practice a working hour system wherein laborers shall work for no more than 8 hours a day and no more than 44 hours on average per week.

Article 38 stipulates that the employer shall guarantee that its laborers have at least one day off per week.

Article 40 stipulates that the employer shall arrange holidays for laborers in accordance with the law during the following holidays:
1. New Year's Day;
2. Spring Festival;
3. International Labor Day;
4. National Day;
5. Other holidays prescribed by laws and regulations.

## Labor Dispute Resolution
Article 5 of the "Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that labor disputes may be resolved through the following approaches:
1. Consultation between the parties;
2. Application to a mediation organization for mediation;
3. Application to a labor dispute arbitration committee for arbitration;
4. Initiation of a lawsuit in the people's court if dissatisfied with the arbitration award, unless otherwise provided for in this Law.

Article 27 stipulates that the limitation period for applying for arbitration of a labor dispute shall be one year. The limitation period shall be calculated from the date when the party concerned knows or should have known that its rights have been infringed upon.

## Termination and Ending of Labor Contracts
Article 36 of the "Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that a labor contract may be terminated by mutual agreement between the employer and the employee.

Article 38 stipulates that the employee may terminate the labor contract if the employer has any of the following circumstances:
1. Fails to provide labor protection or working conditions as stipulated in the labor contract;
2. Fails to pay labor remuneration in full and on time;
3. Fails to pay social insurance premiums for the employee in accordance with the law;
4. The employer's rules and regulations violate the provisions of laws and regulations, thereby harming the employee's rights and interests;
5. The labor contract is rendered invalid due to the circumstances specified in the first paragraph of Article 26 of this Law;
6. Other circumstances in which the employee may terminate the labor contract as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

Article 47 stipulates that economic compensation shall be paid to the employee based on the number of years worked with the employer at the rate of one month's wage for each full year worked. Any period of not less than six months but less than one year shall be counted as one year; any period of less than six months shall be paid at the rate of half a month's wage.
