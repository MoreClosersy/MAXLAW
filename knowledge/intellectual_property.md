# Chinese Intellectual Property Law Provisions

## Basic Copyright Law Provisions
Article 2 of the "Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that works of Chinese citizens, legal persons, or non-legal-person organizations shall enjoy copyright in accordance with this law, regardless of whether they are published or not. Works of foreigners or stateless persons shall be protected by this law in accordance with agreements concluded between their country or habitual residence and China, or international treaties to which both countries are parties.

Article 10 stipulates that copyright includes the following personal and property rights:
1. Right of publication, the right to decide whether to make a work available to the public;
2. Right of authorship, the right to claim authorship and to have the author's name indicated on the work;
3. Right of revision, the right to revise or authorize others to revise a work;
4. Right of integrity, the right to protect a work against distortion and mutilation;
5. Right of reproduction, the right to produce one or more copies of a work by printing, photocopying, rubbing, sound recording, video recording, duplicating, or other means;
6. Right of distribution, the right to provide the original or copies of a work to the public by selling or donating;
7. Right of rental, the right to authorize others to temporarily use the original or copies of a cinematographic work or a work created by a process analogous to cinematography, or computer software, for valuable consideration;
8. Right of exhibition, the right to publicly display the original or copies of a work of fine art or photography;
9. Right of performance, the right to publicly perform a work and to publicly transmit the performance of a work by various means;
10. Right of projection, the right to publicly present a work of fine art, photography, cinematography, or a work created by a process analogous to cinematography through projector, slide projector, or other technical equipment;
11. Right of broadcasting, the right to publicly broadcast or rebroadcast a work by wire or wireless means;
12. Right of information network dissemination, the right to make a work available to the public by wire or wireless means, so that the public may access the work from a place and at a time individually chosen by them;
13. Right of cinematography, the right to fix a work on a medium by cinematography or a process analogous to cinematography;
14. Right of adaptation, the right to change a work to create a new work with originality;
15. Right of translation, the right to translate a work from one language into another;
16. Right of compilation, the right to compile a work or parts of a work into a new work by selection or arrangement;
17. Other rights to be enjoyed by copyright owners.

## Basic Patent Law Provisions
Article 2 of the "Patent Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that "invention-creations" refers to inventions, utility models, and designs. "Invention" refers to a new technical solution proposed for a product, a process, or the improvement thereof. "Utility model" refers to a new technical solution proposed for the shape, structure, or their combination of a product, which is fit for practical use. "Design" refers to a new design of the shape, pattern, or their combination, or the combination of color with shape and pattern, of a product, which creates an aesthetic feeling and is fit for industrial application.

Article 22 stipulates that patents shall be granted for inventions and utility models that possess novelty, inventiveness, and practical applicability. Novelty means that the invention or utility model shall not belong to the prior art, nor has any entity or individual filed before the filing date with the patent administrative department under the State Council an application on an identical invention or utility model which was recorded in patent application documents or patent documents published after the said filing date. Inventiveness means that, compared with the prior art, the invention has prominent substantive features and represents notable progress, and the utility model has substantive features and represents progress. Practical applicability means that the invention or utility model can be manufactured or used and can produce positive results.

## Basic Trademark Law Provisions
Article 3 of the "Trademark Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that registered trademarks include trademarks for goods, service trademarks, collective trademarks, and certification trademarks; trademark registrants shall enjoy exclusive rights to use trademarks, which shall be protected by law.

Article 8 stipulates that any sign capable of distinguishing the goods or services of one natural person, legal person, or other organization from those of others, including words, graphics, letters, numbers, three-dimensional signs, color combinations, sounds, and combinations of the above elements, may be applied for registration as a trademark.

Article 13 stipulates that a holder of a trademark well-known to the relevant public may request for protection of the well-known trademark in accordance with this Law if they believe that their rights have been infringed upon. Where a trademark applied for registration for identical or similar goods is a reproduction, imitation, or translation of another person's well-known trademark not registered in China, which may lead to confusion, it shall be rejected and prohibited from use.

## Trade Secret Protection Provisions
Article 9 of the "Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China" stipulates that a business operator shall not commit the following acts of infringing upon trade secrets:
1. Acquiring trade secrets from a right holder by theft, bribery, fraud, coercion, electronic intrusion, or other improper means;
2. Disclosing, using, or allowing others to use the trade secrets acquired from a right holder by the means mentioned in the preceding paragraph;
3. Disclosing, using, or allowing others to use trade secrets in their possession, in violation of confidentiality obligations or the requirements of the right holder for keeping the trade secrets confidential;
4. Abetting, inducing, or assisting others to violate confidentiality obligations or the requirements of the right holder for keeping trade secrets confidential, in order to acquire, disclose, use, or allow others to use the right holder's trade secrets.

Article 17 stipulates that a business operator who causes damage to others by engaging in unfair competition shall bear civil liability in accordance with the law. The amount of compensation for the damage caused to a business operator by unfair competition shall be determined on the basis of the actual loss suffered by the business operator as a result of the infringement; where it is difficult to calculate the actual loss, the amount shall be determined on the basis of the benefits gained by the infringer from the infringement.
